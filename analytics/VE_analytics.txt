1 Structure
1.1 Directory
analytics   ┬app_state  ┬dau.log
            │           └mau.log
            └user       ┬AAAAAA ┬profile.txt
                        │       ├posts.log
                        │       ├streaks.log
                        │       └tasks.csv
                        ├AAAAAB ┬posts.log
                        ⋮       ⋮

1.2 caption
dau.log: number of Daily Active Users
mau.log: number of Monthly Active Users
profile.txt: profile updated by themselves or automatically, and states LLM caculated
posts.log: log of posts
streaks.log: log of streaks, and current streak
tasks.csv

2. File examples
2.1 dau.log
<ddmmyyyy>: <number>
06-05-2026: 20
07-05-2026: 24
08-05-2026: 19

2.2 mau.log
<mmyyyy>: <number>
04-2026: 72
05-2026: 89

2.3 profile.txt
sign-up date: 26-04-2026
age: blank
gender: blank
job: blank
follower: 8
tasks: Run, Study, Cold shower, Nutrition
monthly active rate: 0.82
persona: blank

[NOTE]
Monthly active rate is updated at the end of every month.
Persona is made with age, gender, job, monthly active rate, and other files (posts.log, streaks.log) by LLM or developer.

2.4 posts.log
<task name>         <posted time>               <number of received reactions>  <comments>
Run                 06-05-2026 06:12            43
Run                 07-05-2026 06:33            69                              new record!
Nutrition           07-05-2026 17:02            23
Run                 07-05-2026 07:01            55                              rainy...

[NOTE]
All of objects are filled all at once.

2.5 streaks.log
<updated time>  <streak>    <action>
28-04-2026      1           break
01-05-2026      1           shield
03-05-2026      3           break
08-05-2026      6           break

[NOTE]
Streaks are recorded when streak is over or user uses their streak shield. Current streak is always listed at last.
streak is not broken (that means be not reset), if they use shield.

2.6 tasks.log
<time stamp>     <action>   <task name>
29-04-2026 18:28 add        Run
29-04-2026 18:29 add        study
01-05-2026 14:28 add        Drink water
03-05-2026 18:01 delete     Drink water
03-05-2026 18:03 add        Nutrition
